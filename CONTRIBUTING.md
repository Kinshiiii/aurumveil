# Contributing

Thank you for contributing to this project! This is a team-based academic project developed collaboratively.

Please read our [Code of Conduct](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) to ensure a respectful and professional environment.

---

## Getting Started

- Each team member works on their own branch
- Keep your changes clear and focused
- Push your work regularly

---

## Making Changes

- Write clean and readable code
- Keep your changes simple and consistent
- Test your work before submitting

---

## Pull Requests

- Open a Pull Request when your work is ready
- Clearly describe what you have implemented
- Be responsive to feedback and requested changes

---

## Review Process

All contributions are reviewed before being merged.

Approval from the team lead is required to include changes in the main branch.

---

## Team Collaboration

- Communicate changes clearly
- Avoid conflicts by keeping your branch up to date
- Respect the work of other team members

---

## Questions

If you have any questions, contact the team lead or project maintainers.
